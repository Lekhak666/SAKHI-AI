# SAKHI

Sakhi is a web application designed to provide a safe and supportive environment for people seeking help and resources related to mental health and well-being. The platform offers a range of features, including anonymous chat support, access to mental health professionals, and a community forum for sharing experiences and advice.

## Features

- **Anonymous Chat Support**: Users can engage in confidential conversations with trained mental health professionals.
- **Access to Mental Health Professionals**: Users can connect with licensed therapists and counselors.
- **Community Forum**: A space for users to share their experiences and support each other.
